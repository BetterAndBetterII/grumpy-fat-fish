胖鱼不好惹 · 隐私政策

适用于 iOS App「胖鱼不好惹」（Grumpy Fat Fish）· 开发者：大肥鱼造物所 · 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 18 日

一句话说明

「胖鱼不好惹」是一个完全离线的单机游戏。我们不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息，也不做任何跟踪。

我们不收集的数据

  • App 不需要注册或登录，没有账号体系。
  • App 不联网：游戏全部内容都打包在 App 里，运行时不会向任何服务器发送请求，我们也没有自己的服务器。
  • App 不包含广告、统计分析、崩溃上报或任何第三方 SDK。
  • App 不读取你的通讯录、位置、相册内容、麦克风或摄像头。

保存在你手机上的数据

游戏进度（贝壳、解锁的角色、强化等级、最佳成绩、设置开关等）只保存在你这台设备的 App 存储里，不会离开设备。删除 App 会一并删除这些数据。

相册（仅在你主动点击时）

结算页的「存到相册」会把这一局的战绩海报保存到你的相册。iOS 会先询问你是否允许 App「添加照片」；App 只会添加这一张图片，不会读取你的相册。你可以随时在「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片」里修改这项权限。

分享（仅在你主动点击时）

结算页的「发给好友」会打开 iOS 系统分享面板，把战绩海报交给你选择的 App。之后的处理遵循你所选 App 的隐私政策，我们不会收到任何信息。

App 内购买（以后如果提供）

现在的版本没有 App 内购买。以后如果提供，付款全部由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策；我们拿不到你的姓名、Apple ID、银行卡等任何支付信息，App 只在你的设备上读取 Apple 返回的购买结果，用来解锁对应内容。

Apple 提供的统计

如果你在 iPhone「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进」里选择了「与 App 开发者共享」，Apple 可能会把不含个人身份的崩溃报告和使用统计提供给开发者。这些数据由 Apple 收集和处理，适用 Apple 的隐私政策，你可以随时关闭。

儿童

App 不收集任何人（包括儿童）的个人信息。

变更与联系

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新这个页面并修改生效日期。有任何问题，请在 GitHub Issues 里留言（需要 GitHub 账号）。Issue 是公开的，请不要在里面写手机号、邮箱等个人信息。

Grumpy Fat Fish · Privacy Policy

Applies to the iOS app “Grumpy Fat Fish” (胖鱼不好惹) · Developer: 大肥鱼造物所 · Effective date: November 18, 2026

In short

Grumpy Fat Fish is a fully offline, single-player game. We do not collect, upload or sell any personal information, and we do not track you.

What we do not collect

  • There is no sign-up or sign-in, and no account of any kind.
  • The app does not connect to the internet: everything the game needs is bundled in the app, it sends no requests to any server while it runs, and we do not operate any servers.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app does not read your contacts, location, photo library, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your phone

Your progress (shells, unlocked fish, upgrade levels, best results, settings) is stored only in the app's storage on your device and never leaves it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Photos (only when you tap)

“Save to Photos” on the results screen saves that run's result poster to your photo library. iOS first asks whether to allow the app to add photos; the app only adds that one image and never reads your library. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Sharing (only when you tap)

“Send to a friend” on the results screen opens the iOS share sheet and hands the result poster to the app you choose. What happens next is governed by that app's privacy policy; we receive nothing.

In-app purchases (if offered in the future)

The current version has no in-app purchases. If they are offered later, payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store under Apple's privacy policy. We never receive your name, Apple Account, card or any other payment information; the app only reads, on your device, the purchase result returned by Apple in order to unlock the content you bought.

Statistics provided by Apple

If you turn on “Share with App Developers” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics, without personal identifiers, with developers. That data is collected and processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and you can turn it off at any time.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. For any question, please open an issue on GitHub Issues (a GitHub account is required). Issues are public, so please do not include your phone number, email address or other personal information.
