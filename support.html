常见问题

怎么操作？
按住屏幕任意位置拖动就能移动，武器会自动攻击；升级时三选一。

存档在哪里？换手机能带走吗？
存档只保存在这台手机的 App 里，不联网、不上传。删除 App 会清除存档；目前不支持跨设备同步。

为什么没有声音？
游戏会跟随手机的静音键；也请检查游戏「设置」里的「音效」「战斗音乐」开关。

没有振动？
在游戏「设置」里打开「振动」，并确认 iPhone「设置 › 声音与触感 › 系统触感反馈」已打开。

「存到相册」失败？
请在 iPhone「设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片 › 胖鱼不好惹」里允许「仅添加照片」。

「排行榜」在哪？
这个版本没有在线排行榜，「我的记录」会记下你在这台手机上的最佳生存时间和单局击杀。

游戏里是什么语言？
游戏内文字目前只有简体中文。

FAQ

How do I play?
Touch anywhere and drag to swim. Your weapons attack automatically; pick one of three upgrades at every level-up.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?
Progress is saved only in the app on this phone. Nothing is uploaded and the game never goes online. Deleting the app deletes your progress; syncing between devices is not supported yet.

Why is there no sound?
The game follows your phone's silent switch. Also check the sound effects and battle music switches in the game's Settings.

No vibration?
Turn on vibration in the game's Settings, and make sure Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics is on.

“Save to Photos” failed?
Allow “Add Photos Only” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › 胖鱼不好惹 (the app’s name on your Home Screen).

Where is the leaderboard?
This version has no online leaderboard. “My Records” keeps your best survival time and most kills in a run on this phone.

What language is the game in?
The in-game text is currently in Simplified Chinese only.
